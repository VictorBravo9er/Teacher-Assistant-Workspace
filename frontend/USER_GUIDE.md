# Teach&Learn — User Guide

Welcome to **Teach&Learn**, the next-generation workspace for educators. This guide will help you navigate the application, set up your classes, and harness the power of AI to supercharge your classroom evaluation and reporting.

## Overview
Teach&Learn allows you to:
- **Organize Class Resources**: Keep syllabi, assignments, and student rosters organized in dedicated, easily searchable class projects.
- **Automate Student Evaluation**: Rate student submissions against your rubrics and track academic growth over time.
- **Generate Instant Progress Reports**: Ask natural questions about any student's performance and receive clear, actionable summaries and insights.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Sign In / Sign Up
- Navigate to the application and click **Sign In** or **Get Started Free**.
- Create an account with your Name, Email, and Phone Number (for notifications/security).
- Once logged in, you will be taken to your **Dashboard**.

### The Sidebar
The left sidebar is your main navigation hub:
- **Active Classes**: Your ongoing classes and courses.
- **Teacher Templates**: Pre-configured class structures you can reuse.
- **Preferences**: Customize your system settings, including Light/Dark mode.
- **User Profile**: Access your account settings and subscription details.

---

## 2. Managing Your Classes

### Creating a New Class
1. Click the **+** (Plus) icon next to "Active Classes" in the sidebar.
2. Enter your Class Name.
3. You can either select an existing Institute or register a new one.
4. Click **Create** to initialize your workspace.

### Class Views
Once inside a class, you can toggle between three view modes using the top navigation bar:
- **Details Only**: Focus entirely on your class configuration and materials.
- **Split View**: Work on class details while keeping the AI Chat open on the side.
- **Chat Only**: Expand the AI Chat to full screen for deep-dive analysis.

---

## 3. Configuring Your Class

Click **Edit** in the top navigation bar to configure your class. Remember to click **Save** when you're done!

### Class Profile
- **Teaching Style**: Briefly describe how you teach (e.g., Socratic, project-based).
- **Assessment Preferences**: Define how you grade (e.g., rubrics, qualitative feedback).
- **Special Notes**: Add any critical reminders for this specific class.

### Materials Repository
Upload your syllabus, reading materials, assignment briefs, and rubrics. The AI will use these documents to understand the context of your class.

### AI Instructions & Guidelines
Provide custom prompt guidelines to the AI. For example, "Always map student performance to the learning objectives in the syllabus."

---

## 4. Roster & Student Management

Keep track of your students in the **Student Roster** section.
- **Add Students**: Click **Add Student** to enroll them in the class.
- **Student Portfolios**: Click on any student card to open their detailed portfolio. Here, you can review their submissions, grades, and individualized feedback.

---

## 5. RAG AI Diagnostics & Chat

The core of Teach&Learn is your AI teaching assistant.
- **Start a Session**: Click **New AI Analysis** to start a chat session.
- **Ask Questions**: Use the chat input to ask the AI questions like:
  - *"Generate a progress report for John Doe based on his last three assignments."*
  - *"How does the class generally perform on the mid-term rubric?"*
  - *"Draft a lesson plan for next week based on the uploaded syllabus."*
- **Manage History**: Search past sessions, duplicate them, or delete them as needed.

---

## 6. Pro Tips & Shortcuts

- **Command Palette**: Press `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K` on Mac) to quickly jump between classes, templates, and settings.
- **Templates**: Have a setup you love? Click **Save Active as Template** in the sidebar to reuse your class configuration (profile, rubrics, AI guidelines) for future semesters.
